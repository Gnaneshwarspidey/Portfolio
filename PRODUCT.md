A personal developer portfolio establishing credibility, showcasing technical depth, and converting visitors into interview conversations and professional collaborations through direct evidence of working software, data pipelines, and verified credentials.

An applied AI/ML engineer who bridges intelligent models (computer vision, ML pipelines, deep learning) with robust, clean full-stack web architectures. Emphasizes production-ready implementation, measurable real-world utility, and clean code over purely theoretical or prototype-only models.
